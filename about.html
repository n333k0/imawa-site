About

15+ years of originalmusic and sound.

Founded by Agustín Iacona and Ary Werthein, IMAWA has spent 15+ years composing original music and sound for film, television and advertising.

Our work blends orchestral scoring, electronic production and sound design — built to carry the emotional weight of a story, a brand or a broadcast moment, without losing precision. We approach every project the way our clients need their audio to work: we listen, we understand, we design, we test, we deliver.

From sonic branding for broadcasters to scores for streaming platforms and commercials, IMAWA's work has been recognized across the industry.

AI is part of how we work now — a sidekick, not a shortcut. It helps us move faster and explore further. Our craft still makes the final call.

Every piece of music we compose becomes part of an original catalog, cleared and licensed with full clarity — no ambiguity, even in the age of artificially generated audio.
